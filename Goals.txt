!Goal Ideas

Mortals:

The Matriarch/Patriarch
	Grow your Legacy
	T1: Have a spouse with at least 80 relationship and 3 Children
	T2: Have at least 80 relationship with 6 children, and have at least 1 grandchild
	T2: Have at least 15 grandchildren all with wealth 30 or above

The Socialite
	Mingle man!
	T1: Have at least 70 appearance, 2 lovers, and 10 friends
	T2: Have at least 5 lovers, and 5 friends in high places (judge, lawyer, politician, celebrity, etc)
	and 1 best friend
	T3: 5 lovers, 8 friends in high places, 2 best friends, and 100 appearance
	
The Book Worm
	Smartz
	T1: Earn a bachelors degree, 60 Intelligence
	T2: Earn a Masters, 80 Intelligence
	T3 (hidden for mortals, requires archivist/linguist combo): Study translated ancient relic, 100 int

Both/Hybrid:

The Cunning Linguist
	Master languages 
	T1: Fluent in 3
	T2: Fluent in 10
	T3 COMBO Archivist: Fluent in Ancient language (Latin, Old Norse, Old English, Etc)
	and translate an ancient tablet/relic
 
The Archivist
	Collect Unique Books or Relics
	T1: 3 Unique Books/relics
	T2: Construct a Library and house 10 Unique Books/relics
	T3 COMBO Linguist: Fluent in Ancient language (Latin, Old Norse, Old English, Etc)
	and translate an ancient tablet/relic
