The Midnight Racket
		For three consecutive nights, a pack of stray hounds has gathered in the cobblestone alley behind your residence. They aren't just
		howling at the moon; they are snarling and barking violently. The relentless noise is fraying your nerves and ruining your sleep.
		
		Option 1: Drive them off (Prowess check)
			You light a heavy iron lantern, grab a heavy walking stick, and march down into the freezing alley to scatter the mutts yourself.

			Success: You swing the lantern, and the firelight scatters the pack. Investigating the area they were barking at, you find no one—but you do find a strange puddle of thick, dark blood that is freezing unnaturally fast. 

			Failure: The pack is panicked and desperate. They turn on you, tearing through your coat and leaving a nasty bite on your calf before you manage to retreat back inside. (-15 Health)

		Option 2: Shutter the windows and Endure
			Result: You stay physically safe, but the psychological toll of the snarling beasts—and the strange scratching noises that follow—leaves you deeply unsettled for the rest of the year. (-10 Health)
		
		Option 3: Dismiss the dogs [Requires Animalism trait]
			Result: The dogs instantly bow away from you and scatter back into the night. You notice a strange puddle of thick, dark blood that is freezing unnaturally fast.

The Crimson Tonic
		A brightly painted wagon has rolled into the market square, drawing a massive crowd. A man introducing himself as "Dr. Silas Vane" stands on the buckboard. He is charismatic, unnaturally pale, and wears dark tinted spectacles despite the overcast sky. He is peddling a "Miracle Panacea" guaranteed to balance the humors, cure the wasting sickness, and restore youthful vigor. The vial he holds catches the dim light—it is filled with a thick, almost luminous crimson syrup that smells faintly of copper and iron.
		
		Option 1: Purchase and Consume the Tonic [-10 wealth, silently checks Insight >20]
			You pay the exoribtant fee and swallow the heavy metallic sludge.

			Success: The effects are immediate and terrifyingly potent. Your cough vanishes, your joints stop aching, and you feel a surge of raw, physical power. However, for the rest of the year, normal food tastes like ash. (+40 Health, +5 Prowess)
			
			Failure: Your mortal stomach violently rejects the thick, unnatural liquid. You spend three agonizing days bedridden, sweating through your sheets and hallucinating shadows moving in the corners of your room. (-15 Health)
			
		Option 2: Keep your coin and walk away.
		You refuse to be swindled by a traveling charlatan. You pull your coat tight and head home.

			Result: A week later, you hear that the elderly, bedridden baker who bought the tonic is completely cured of his severe arthritis. Those who shop at his bakery tell of his new oddities.
		
		Option 3: Investigate the Tonic [Requires The Uncanny Trait]
			Your heightened intuition spots something peculiar, the tonic is mixed with blood.
			
			Result: The Doctor locks eyes with you, and gives you a small knowing smile. He places a vial in your hands and asks for your benevolence in the nights to come. You drink the vial and gain +40 Health, +5 Prowess).
		
The Headmaster's Office
		
		While doing chores, you notice the Headmaster has left the door to his private study ajar. Sitting on his 
		mahogany desk is a [Mysterious Relic]—a heavy looking, strangely shaped silver and deep crimson relic. 
		Stealing it would be incredibly dangerous, as the Headmaster is known for his brutal discipline.

		Option 1: Leave it be (Event Ends)
			You aren't foolish enough to risk the Headmaster's wrath so soon after starting school. You walk away, the faint
			hum of the object fading from your mind as you return to your duties. 

		Option 2: Steal the Relic (Continues to The Inquisition)
			The relic vanishes into your pockets a moment later, but you accidentally knock over the inkwell behind the relic.
			You slip quietly down the corridor and take your seat in  the main hall, masking your racing heart with a 
			cheerful, innocent smile. You furiously scrub your hand on a rag to no avail, the ink stains you.
			(+3 Cunning, +1 Mysterious Relic)

		The Inquisition
			Less than five minutes later, the heavy doors of the main hall slam shut. The Headmaster locks the deadbolt and

			He begins a slow, methodical march down the central aisle, demanding everyone empty their pockets onto their desks.
