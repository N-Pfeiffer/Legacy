Hobby - Description
Botany - (Gathering, Crafting Foraging herbs, craft herbal remedies, grow and study plants
    Alchemy[Requires Botany 50] - (Crafting) Combine a wide range of ingredients into specific potions and concoctions
Metallurgy - (Gathering, Crafting) Gather Ores, Scavenge metals, smith weapons, tools and armor
    Artificing [Requires Metallurgy 50, Mysticism 25] - (Crafting) Create jewelry, unique devices, or even mystical artifacts
Hunting - (Gathering) Hunt and track wildlife, skin pelts
    Stalking [Requires 50 Hunting] - Stalk and kill people and monsters, spy on NPC's 
Tailoring - (Crafting) Use leathers, skins, pelts, and plant fibers to create clothing, armor, and other fabrics
Scholarship - (Crafting/Int) Translate languages, study relics, and create tomes
Mysticism - Read the stars, interpret the omens, use artifacts, and even commune with spirits
Angling - (Gathering and Health) Fish the waters of Brittania and ease your stress
Performance - (Charisma) Play instruments, dance like a refined member of the elite
