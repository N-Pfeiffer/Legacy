		A vampires Domain (and disciplines) is much different from a mortals career (and education). Lets make these a completely seperate tab

		logically. 

		

		For Domain, while it is still primarily based around wealth, Domain opens up mechanically much more. When the player becomes a

		vampire, they lose their career (vampires cant work day jobs). Their career and rank will give them starting bonuses in their domain.

		Within the domain tab, the vampire will have two things, their Haven and their spheres of ineluence.

		

		For the Haven:

		To buy a Haven, players first need to secure wealth through their spheres of influence. After meeting the required wealth threshold for

		the desired Haven, they can buy/develop it over a few years. Buldings will have unique art so that players can in a way design their

		haven and the building/room layout.

		1.High class Townhouse: Very expensive, Compact space(transitions to penthouse later). Vibe is velvety, mahogany, high society

		2. Crypt Cellar: Medium cost, underground, hybrid stone and dirt tunnels. Vibe is stone, ancient, iron, catacombs

		3. Private Estate: Expensive, Largest buildable area, hybrid interior/exterior features. Vibe is granduer, overgrown ivy, privacy

		4. The Warren: Cheap sprawling underground. Vibe: Filthy, Secretive, Eyes in the dark, Dangerous

		

		

		For spheres of influence:

		The flavor of these will have to apply to each era, so they must remain high level descriptors I think.

		1.High Society (+Wealth, +influence, +Suspicion, High level control enables the decision to host a private event)

		2. Merchants (++Wealth, +influence, high level control enables the decision to buy possessions and relics)

		3. Law (-Suspcision, -Wealth, High level control unlocks decision to immeditely reduce suspicion but reduces control level of Law)

		4. Industry (+Wealth, High Control gives speed bonus to constructing Haven buildings)

		5. The Press (-Suspicion, High control unlocks decision to manipulate control of other spheres at the cost of Press control)

		6. The Church (-Suspicion, +Resonance)

		7. The Academy (++Resonance, small chance for Relics every 5 years)

		8. The Underworld (+wealth +Suspicion, high level control unlocks various plots)
